## Schedule notes

Session times follow the main conference website. Tito and RevenueCat list different overall event hours. Paired talks share a block; separate start times are not published.

Two additions come from indexed copies of social posts:

- **Eric Schofield’s talk:** the conference’s November 3 post names it “An iOS Developer’s Guide to Surviving the Appocolypse.” The website still shows a placeholder. [Conference feed](https://teapawt.org/swiftsonicconf).
- **Bitrig open mic:** Adrian Eves’s October 29 post lists Saturday, November 21, **5:30–6:30 PM**, with Betsy Langowski and the Bitrig team. It is absent from the main timetable. [Copy of the post](https://xitter.gq/ricketson_).

Confirm the open mic details with the organizers. Speaker categories follow the website’s lineup. Danielle Lewis’s profile follows her published biography rather than the conflicting subtitle.
