# About Swiftsonic

A Swift and iOS conference founded by Adrian Eves in Nashville.

## Overview

Adrian Eves started Swiftsonic as a place for indie developers to meet and learn between WWDC and the next conference season. 2026 is its first year.

The music festival format pairs emerging speakers with experienced headliners. Some give separate talks in one block; others present together.

Openers could also opt into mentorship and work on their talks with a headliner.
